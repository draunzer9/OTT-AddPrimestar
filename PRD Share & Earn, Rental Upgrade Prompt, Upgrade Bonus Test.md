# PRD: Share & Earn, Rental Upgrade Prompt, and Upgrade Bonus Test

**Status:** Draft for review **Owner:** Product **Scope:** One market, 60-day pilot **Currency:** INR. All prices and costs are placeholders to replace with real data.

## 1. Problem

Basic (ad-tier) users generate ad revenue but are the largest pool of potential ad-free subscribers. Two gaps limit growth:

1. Viewers have no reason to bring new users to the platform, so acquisition relies on paid marketing.
2. Frequent renters pay repeatedly for single titles without seeing that a subscription would cost less.

## 2. Goals

- Increase new paid subscriptions from existing viewers.
- Increase basic-to-ad-free upgrades.
- Keep the paywall intact: non-subscribers must subscribe to watch the full title.
- Keep reward and promotion costs below the revenue they generate.

## 3. Non-goals

- Points for watching ads (conflicts with Share & Earn on the same ad inventory).
- Gamified ad-skip.
- Free rentals for existing ad-free subscribers.
- Rent-to-unlock-3-days mechanic.
- Telecom bundle (separate business-development track).

## 4. Success metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target for pilot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referred subscriptions per 100 shares | New paid subs attributed to a share link, per 100 shares | Set after first 2 weeks; must make reward cost pay back |
| Reward cost ratio | Total rewards / referred ad + subscription revenue | Below 15% |
| Prompt upgrade rate | Upgrades from users shown the prompt / users shown the prompt | At least 3%, and above the holdout |
| Bonus incremental lift | Upgrade rate (prompt + bonus) minus upgrade rate (prompt only) | At least 0.25 percentage points to justify cost |
| 30-day retention of referred friends | Share of referred subscribers still active at day 30 | Not below the platform average |

## 5. Feature A: Share & Earn

**User story:** As a viewer, I can share a title link with a friend and earn a reward when that friend watches through my link or subscribes.

**Requirements**

- A Share button on every title page creates a link with a referral ID tied to the sharer's account.
- The link shows the title, poster, and a Watch button.
- Non-subscribers hit the subscription screen before playback. The referral ID is kept through signup and checkout.
- Subscribers go straight to playback with ads.
- Reward rules:
  - **Ad-tier revenue share:** 15% of the ad revenue generated by the referred friend's ad-tier viewing.
  - **Subscription bonus:** a flat ₹20 when a referred friend starts a paid subscription.
- Only completed ad views count. Only the first full view of each title per friend counts. Repeat views earn nothing.
- Caps: maximum ₹300 per referrer per month. Maximum one subscription bonus per friend account.
- Eligibility: one reward-eligible account per device and payment method. Phone OTP verification required.
- Vesting: rewards credit after the friend stays active for 30 days.
- Payouts are manual approval during the pilot. Flagged accounts go to review.

**Tracking events:** link\_created, link\_opened, paywall\_shown, subscription\_started, subscription\_completed, ad\_view\_completed (with referral ID), reward\_accrued, reward\_vested.

**Out of scope for pilot:** automated payouts, public leaderboard, multi-level referrals.

## 6. Feature B: Rental-based upgrade prompt (basic users)

**User story:** As a basic-tier viewer who rents often, I see when an ad-free subscription would cost me less than my rentals.

**Trigger**

- User is on the basic (ad) tier.
- User has made 3 or more rentals in the current calendar month, excluding titles in the subscription library.
- User has not seen this prompt in the last 30 days.

**Message:** "You've rented \[N\] movies this month for ₹\[total\]. Ad-free unlimited viewing is ₹149/month." Show the Upgrade button and a Not now option.

**Rules**

- Ad-free users never see the prompt.
- The prompt shows the user's actual spend, not a generic claim.
- Dismissal is recorded and suppresses the prompt for 30 days.

**Tracking events:** prompt\_shown, prompt\_clicked, prompt\_dismissed, upgrade\_completed (with prompt source).

## 7. Feature C: Upgrade bonus A/B test

**User story:** As a basic-tier viewer who upgrades after the prompt, I receive one free new-release rental.

**Design**

- Eligible users: those who see the Feature B prompt.
- Randomized into two arms at the user level:
  - **Arm 1 (prompt only):** sees the prompt, no bonus.
  - **Arm 2 (prompt + bonus):** sees the prompt, and on upgrade receives one free new-release rental, capped at a licensing cost of ₹60 per redemption.
- Randomization is fixed for the test period. Users stay in their arm.
- Test runs for the full 60-day pilot.

**Rules**

- Bonus is limited to new releases outside the subscription library.
- One bonus per user, valid for 7 days.
- The bonus is never offered on the subscription screen to users outside the test.

## 8. Other features in the prototype (supporting the goal)

These are not part of this PRD's scope, but they are on the prototype roadmap:

- **Annual plan toggle:** "Pay for 10 months, get 12." Annual price ₹1,490 versus ₹1,788 for 12 monthly payments (saves ₹298, about 16.7%).
- **Resume watching across devices:** supports retention and session frequency.
- **Smart ad frequency:** a controlled test only, because it trades ad impressions for retention.

## 9. Calculations

### 9.1 Basic assumptions

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Ad-tier ARPU (ad revenue per user per month) | ₹40 |
| Ad-free price | ₹149/month |
| Upgrade margin per user per month (149 − 40) | ₹109 |
| Monthly churn, ad-free | 8% |
| Monthly churn, ad-free with improvement | 6% |
| Average months a referred ad-tier friend stays | 3 |
| Licensing cost per free rental | ₹60 |

### 9.2 Scenario 1: Share & Earn, one referred ad-tier friend

- Ad revenue: ₹40 × 3 months = ₹120.
- Reward: 15% × ₹120 = ₹18.
- Platform keeps ₹102 of ₹120 (85%).

### 9.3 Scenario 2: Share & Earn, one referred friend who subscribes

- Subscription revenue: ₹149 × 3 months = ₹447.
- Reward: ₹20 flat bonus. No ad-tier revenue share, since the friend is not on the ad tier.
- Platform keeps ₹427 of ₹447 (95.5%).

### 9.4 Scenario 3: Share & Earn, a referrer's 10 shares

Assumptions: 1 of 10 friends subscribes, 4 of 10 watch on the ad tier, 5 of 10 don't convert.

- Subscription revenue: 1 × ₹447 = ₹447.
- Ad-tier revenue: 4 × ₹120 = ₹480.
- Gross platform revenue: ₹927.
- Rewards: subscription bonus 1 × ₹20 = ₹20, plus ad-tier share 4 × ₹18 = ₹72. Total ₹92.
- Reward cost ratio: ₹92 / ₹927 = 9.9%, below the 15% target.

### 9.5 Scenario 4: Share & Earn, cap check

A highly active referrer sending 100 shares a month with the same conversion mix would earn about ₹920 before caps. The ₹300 monthly cap limits this to ₹300, so the platform's maximum exposure per referrer is ₹300.

### 9.6 Scenario 5: Rental upgrade prompt, monthly impact

Assumptions: 5,000 basic users see the prompt each month, 4% upgrade.

- Upgrades: 5,000 × 4% = 200.
- Monthly margin added: 200 × ₹109 = ₹21,800.
- Cost: none beyond the messaging system. This is why the prompt launches to all eligible users.

### 9.7 Scenario 6: Upgrade bonus test, first-month view

Assumptions: 2,000 users per arm.

| Arm | Upgrades | Bonus cost | Monthly margin from upgrades |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prompt only | 4% × 2,000 = 80 | ₹0 | 80 × ₹109 = ₹8,720 |
| Prompt + bonus | 5% × 2,000 = 100 | 100 × ₹60 = ₹6,000 | 100 × ₹109 = ₹10,900 |

Incremental margin: ₹10,900 − ₹8,720 = ₹2,180, against ₹6,000 in bonus cost. In the first month alone, the bonus does not pay back. Use the lifetime view in 9.8.

### 9.8 Scenario 7: Upgrade bonus test, lifetime view

- Lifetime margin per upgrader: ₹109 ÷ 0.08 = ₹1,362.50.
- Incremental upgrades in Arm 2: 100 − 80 = 20.
- Incremental lifetime value: 20 × ₹1,362.50 = ₹27,250.
- Bonus cost: ₹6,000.
- Net: ₹21,250 in favor of the bonus.

**Break-even:** ₹6,000 ÷ ₹1,362.50 ≈ 4.4, so about 5 incremental upgrades per 2,000 users (0.25 percentage points). Below that lift, drop the bonus.

### 9.9 Scenario 8: Sample size for the bonus test

To detect a 1-point lift (4% vs 5%) with 80% power at 5% significance, each arm needs about 6,700 users. The 2,000-per-arm plan in 9.7 is underpowered for a 1-point lift. Either extend the test population to about 6,700 per arm (13,400 total), or accept that only a larger lift can be detected. Confirm the eligible population size before launch.

### 9.10 Scenario 9: Churn improvement value

- LTV at 8% churn: ₹109 ÷ 0.08 = ₹1,362.50 margin per upgrader.
- LTV at 6% churn: ₹109 ÷ 0.06 = ₹1,816.67 margin per upgrader.
- Gain per upgrader: about ₹454.

Cutting churn is worth more than most acquisition gains, so churn tracking should be part of the pilot dashboard.

### 9.11 Scenario 10: Annual plan

- 12 monthly payments: ₹149 × 12 = ₹1,788.
- Annual plan: ₹149 × 10 = ₹1,490.
- Customer saving: ₹298 (16.7%).
- Platform receives ₹1,490 upfront, which improves cash flow, but revenue per year drops by ₹298 per annual subscriber. Annual subscribers must stay longer than the monthly baseline to justify the discount. Track annual-plan churn separately.

## 10. Experiment and guardrails

- **Stopping rule:** stop an arm early if reward cost ratio exceeds 15% or if complaints or fraud flags rise above a set threshold.
- **Cannibalization check:** monitor whether ad-tier revenue from non-referred users drops during the pilot.
- **Fraud checks:** flag many new signups from one device or IP range, unusual session patterns, and one referrer generating most of a friend group's activity.
- **Holdouts:** keep a holdout of eligible users who see neither feature, to measure the total effect.

## 11. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Fake accounts farm rewards | Phone OTP, one account per device and payment method, 30-day vesting, manual approval |
| Share links bring users who would have subscribed anyway | Compare referred conversion with a holdout of non-shared traffic |
| Rental prompt lowers rental revenue | Track rental revenue for prompted users; the upgrade margin must exceed lost rental revenue |
| Licensing restrictions on some titles | Legal review before enabling sharing or free rentals for any title |
| Bonus test underpowered | Use the sample size in 9.9 before launch |

## 12. Open questions

1. Current conversion rate of shared links and share of non-subscriber views.
2. Actual ad-tier ARPU by market and tier.
3. Rental revenue per user and licensing cost per title.
4. Eligible population size for the bonus test.
5. Which titles carry sharing, revenue-split, or rental restrictions.

## 13. Pilot timeline

- **Weeks 1–2:** tracking foundation (share links, events, dashboard).
- **Weeks 3–4:** rental upgrade prompt live for all eligible basic users.
- **Weeks 4–5:** Share & Earn live with manual payouts, plus the bonus test arms.
- **Weeks 6–10:** run the pilot. Review the dashboard weekly.
- **Week 10:** decision review. Keep, adjust, or drop each feature based on the metrics above.
